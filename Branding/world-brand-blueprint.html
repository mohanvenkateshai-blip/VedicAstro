01
COSMIC FOUNDATIONS
Core Strategy

Define the soul of your brand. This phase establishes the strategic foundation that all future decisions inherit from.

• Archetypal Positioning — Define your brand’s mythic role and emotional territory.
• Target Persona Mapping — Deep audience psychology and jobs-to-be-done.
• Competitive Audit — Visual, verbal, and strategic gap analysis.
• Brand DNA Document — Mission, vision, values, promise, and personality.
• Naming & Domain Strategy — Secure memorable, ownable brand assets.
02
VISUAL IDENTITY
Mystical Aesthetics

Translate strategy into a distinctive, ownable visual language that feels inevitable.

• The Cosmic Palette — Primary, secondary, accent, neutral, and semantic colors with accessibility baked in.
• Symbolic Logo Design — Geometric marks, wordmarks, and clearspace rules.
• Typography Pairing — Display + text pairing with precise tracking and optical sizing.
• Design System Creation — Component library, iconography system, motion language.
• Photography & Illustration Direction — Mood, treatment, and usage guidelines.
03
TECHNICAL MAPPING
UX Architecture

Bridge brand strategy with technical execution. Build the scalable infrastructure.

• Chart Layout Engine — Information architecture & user flows.
• Design Tokens — CSS variables, Tailwind config, Figma tokens sync.
• Responsive System — Mobile-first, fluid typography, and spacing scales.
• High-Fidelity Prototyping — Clickable journeys in Figma.
• Accessibility & Performance — WCAG 2.2 AA compliance built in.
04
TONE & CONTENT
Voice Framework

Define the verbal identity that builds deep emotional trust and recognition.

• Brand Voice Guidelines — Archetype, personality traits, do’s and don’ts.
• Messaging Pillars — Core narratives and proof points.
• Terminology Glossary — Approved language for product, marketing, and support.
• Automated Copy Framework — Templates for emails, landing pages, social, and ads.
• Error States & Microcopy — Human, empathetic language in every edge case.
05
ACTIVATION
Go-To-Market

Prepare every channel and asset for launch with precision and coherence.

• Launch Landing Page — High-converting hero + narrative experience.
• Social Media Kit — Templates, carousels, stories, and motion assets.
• Community Building Strategy — Discord, X, LinkedIn, and owned channels.
• Influencer & Analyst Outreach — Narrative-led pitch materials.
• Internal Brand Training — Enable every team member to live the brand.
06
LAUNCH & EVOLUTION
Audit & Growth

Launch with confidence, then continuously evolve based on real-world feedback.

• Launch Audit — Brand consistency checklist across all touchpoints.
• App Store & Marketplace Optimization — Screenshots, descriptions, and visual identity.
• Feedback Loops — Structured user research and brand sentiment tracking.
• Feature & Brand Expansion — Scalable system for new products and markets.
• Continuous Evolution Framework — Quarterly brand health reviews and updates.
